# Hi, I'm Jessica Silva Salgado

### Physical Therapist | Healthtech & Digital Health | Neurorehabilitation & Neurotechnology

I am a healthcare and technology professional based in São Paulo, Brazil, focused on the intersection of **rehabilitation, digital health, and human movement**.

My background combines Physiotherapy, IT support experience, and a degree in Systems Analysis and Development. This interdisciplinary foundation helps me understand both clinical needs and the technology required to translate them into practical, accessible, and patient-centered solutions.

## Professional direction

I am building my career in **healthtech and digital health**, with a long-term research interest in **neurorehabilitation and neurotechnology**.

My goal is to contribute to technologies that improve rehabilitation outcomes, clinical decision-making, access to care, and the experience of patients and healthcare professionals.

- Human-centered and accessible health technology

## Featured project

### [PhysioToPhD](https://github.com/Jessiessalgado/physiotophd)

An interdisciplinary digital health platform connecting physiotherapy, rehabilitation technology, neuroscience, and evidence-based practice.

The project includes a public research-oriented website, blog, protected content management system, Supabase integration, and automated security checks.

**Stack:** TypeScript · React · TanStack Start · Supabase · Tailwind CSS

[Visit the project](https://physiotophd.vercel.app) · [Explore the source code](https://github.com/Jessiessalgado/physiotophd)
